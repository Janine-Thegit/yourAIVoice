Build Your Voice

You already have a voice, built from what you lived through before you ever trained as a coach. Most of it still sits in your body rather than on a page. Milton exists to notice that voice and put words around it, so the coaching you already do can show up in your writing without you having to invent yourself twice.

  1. Palette. Names your natural register.
  2. Values ranking. Reveals which values actually shape your decisions, not the ones you'd list if asked cold.
  3. Five questions. About how you talk when you mean it.
  4. References. Catch the texture underneath.
  5. Dream client. A picture of the client you're actually writing for, specific enough to feel like one person rather than an audience.
  6. Samples. Your own writing, checked against what's emerged so far.

Each stage builds on the one before it, so nothing gets guessed at the end.

Expect around ninety minutes, in one sitting or spread across a few. You'll need honesty more than eloquence. What comes out the other side is a voice guide, built from what you actually said, ready to sit inside an AI project and write the way you would.
