What to do now

Three steps to go live

Five minutes to set up. A lifetime of writing in your own voice.

1

Create a Claude Project

Go to claude.ai, click Projects in the sidebar, and create a new project. Give it your name or your practice name.

2

Paste your voice guide

In the project settings, find Custom Instructions. Paste your voice guide there. Claude will apply it to every conversation in that project.

3

Start writing

Open a chat in your project and ask Claude to write something — a newsletter intro, a social post, an email to a client. It will sound like you.
